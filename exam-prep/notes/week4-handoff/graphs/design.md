# Design
Concept: a paper revision sheet showing the two different graph operations side by side.
Background #f5f3eb; ink #1c302c; programme #1e6851; cash #ad4e2a; original #68766e.
Serif headings, system sans body. Focal elements are the two graphs, with source and interpretation below. Keep graph labels at least 28px at 1920px. Static for study; no decorative motion or audio. Fixed dimensions for the HyperFrames source; the portable worksheet opens a separate SVG for laptop reading.
