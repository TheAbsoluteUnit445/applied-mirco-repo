## Definitions
- **Weight** = average % of a final/resit exam, from the split weight over F24, R25, F25 and R26.
- **Priority A**: in ≥ 3 of the 6 papers, *or* in ≥ 2 finals/resits with ≥ 10% split weight summed.
- **Priority B**: appears sometimes, or as a one-off big block.
- **Priority C**: never examined in a final/resit, so skim.
- **R&G x.y** = chapter x, y-th ▶ section in that chapter. The book does not number its sections.
- **Est. h** = hours to be able to solve the topic cold: redo the exercises, plus the exam Qs, plus the gap reading.
- **Student status:** W1-4 done once (redo all). W5 half done (Ex 5.2-5.4 open). W6 not started (lecture 6 Oct). W7 lecture 13 Oct.

**Reading the table**
- The four real gaps with exam weight are **Ramsey (W6), ban vs laissez-faire, voting/government failure (W7) and the normative/positive one-liners**.
- Ramsey and voting will partly be closed by the W6/W7 sheets. Ban vs laissez-faire and POSNORM will *not* be, so drill them on past exams.
- Everything else is trained by tutorials: the job is to redo them cold and finish Ex 5.2-5.4.
