Criteria for evaluation tax systems:
(Borrowed from Gregory Mankiw: gregmankiw.blogspot.com
1. Efficiency: minimise distortion of individ. choices
(Or in case of externalities: correct these choices)
2. Egalitarianism: more equal after-tax income distribution
(ethical point of view SW-function)
3. Intergenerational equity: revenue should be such that current
generation does not burden future generations too much
government debt; saving for aging of current generation
(4. Stabilisation: dampening of the business cycle
Keynesian policies)
Evaluation of tax systems

Proponents of stabilisation policies:
• Timely increases and decreases in government net
expenditures help to dampen the business cycle
• Helps to maintain production capacity
Opponents:
•
In practice, governments often act to late, which makes that
policies may actually strengthen the business cycle
• Politicians do tend to increase net exp. in ‘bad’ times,
but do not decrease net exp. in ‘good’ times
• Risk of undertaking projects that fail cost-benefit analysis
