# PER-INCOME - Income effects, labour supply (income vs substitution)

**Status:** taught (lecture 4, Set 4) · **Exam weight:** in **2/4** finals/resits (R25, F25; in both consecutive exams) · 5 F/R sub-Qs · **4.1% of a typical exam** (split; #8 overall) · **Priority B** (high B: when it appears it is a 4-6 pt block)

## How the exam asks it

Template **T14, steps 1-4**.

| Exam Q | Type | Content |
|---|---|---|
| [R25 5a](<../../Past exams/Resits/2025-07 Resit (with solutions).pdf>) | Derivation | FOC with concave V: bV′(a + be) − δ = 0 |
| [R25 5b](<../../Past exams/Resits/2025-07 Resit (with solutions).pdf>) | Comparative statics via the FOC | A higher a lowers V′, so the marginal benefit of effort falls and effort falls |
| [R25 5c](<../../Past exams/Resits/2025-07 Resit (with solutions).pdf>) | Verbal (1 sentence) | Concave: the first euros meet basic needs |
| [F25 4a](<../../Past exams/Finals/2025-10 Final (with solutions).pdf>) | Interpretation | W·V′(WH) − X′(24 − H) = 0: the MB of an hour's pay vs the value of lost leisure |
| [F25 4b](<../../Past exams/Finals/2025-10 Final (with solutions).pdf>) | Comparative statics via the FOC | A higher W is ambiguous. Each hour pays more (more hours), but a richer worker values extra income less (fewer hours) |

**Steps:**
1. Write U = V(income) − cost(effort or hours) and take the FOC.
2. Read off MB = wage × V′(income) and MC = the marginal disutility.
3. A change in the base salary works only through V′ (income effect).
4. A change in the wage moves both the "wage ×" factor (substitution) and V′ (income), so the net effect is ambiguous.

**Pitfalls:** "just name-dropping [income/substitution effect] yields no points" (F25 4b). Describe *how the MB changes*. Argue through the FOC term (R25 5b).
