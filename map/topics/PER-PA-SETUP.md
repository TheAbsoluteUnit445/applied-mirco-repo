## How the exam asks it

Never on its own. Every contract question assumes you can write these in one line:
- Y = a + bQ, U = Y − V(E), Π = PQ − Y
- the PC U ≥ U_alt
- the backward-induction order (agent first, then principal)

See [PER-PA-AGENT](PER-PA-AGENT.md) and [PER-PA-PRINC](PER-PA-PRINC.md).
