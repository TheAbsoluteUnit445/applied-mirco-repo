| Exam Q | Type, pts | Solution steps |

| [F25 8b](<../../Past exams/Finals/2025-10 Final (with solutions).pdf>) p.15 | Verbal ≤2 sent., 2 | 1) Pick one positive mechanism: majority voting (the North is outvoted), rent-seeking or lobbying, corruption, or budget-maximising bureaucrats. 2) Link it to *this* case in at most 2 sentences. Efficiency or normative arguments = 0. |

Ready-made "positive explanations" list (for any "why does the government do X inefficient thing?" question):
- median voter / majority tyranny;
- rational ignorance plus concentrated special interests;
- rent-seeking (the lobbying resources are themselves a social cost);
- Niskanen bureaucrat maximising the budget;
- logrolling / pork barrel;
- corruption.

**Pitfalls:** don't slip into a normative or efficiency argument. Name the mechanism *and* apply it.
