# PUB-EXT-PIGOU: Externalities: market vs efficient output, Pigouvian tax/subsidy, DWL

**Status:** taught (W2) | **Exam weight:** 3 of 4 finals/resits (R25, F25, R26) plus midterm M25. Split weight 16.1% summed, **about 4.0% per exam** | **Priority: A**

Exam page numbers (p.) are PDF pages of the solutions file. Templates: see [exams-tagged.md](<../_work/exams-tagged.md>) §3.

## 1. How the exam asks it
Templates: **T2** steps 3 and 5 (linear market + external cost), **T1** step 5 (subsidy for a public good), **T6** step 5 (tax vs subsidy with a binary choice).

| Exam Q | Type, pts | Solution steps |
|---|---|---|
| [F25 9c](<../../Past exams/Finals/2025-10 Final (with solutions).pdf>) p.16 | Numerical, 2 | 1) Efficient Q*: D = S + MEC(Q), with MEC = αQ. 2) Tax = **MEC(Q\*)**, not MEC at the market Q. 3) Algebra gives 180α/(3+α). |
| [R26 3e](<../../Past exams/Resits/2026-07 Resit (with solutions).pdf>) p.8 | Numerical, 2 | 1 pt for Q* = 90 from D = S + MEC, 1 pt for tax = MEC(90) = 45. |
| [R26 3d](<../../Past exams/Resits/2026-07 Resit (with solutions).pdf>) p.8 | Numerical, 2 | 1) Welfare without the tax: CS + PS − EC. 2) With the €30 tax: CS + PS + **revenue** − EC. 3) Compare: +800, so the tax raises efficiency (it acts as a partial Pigouvian tax). |
| [R26 3a](<../../Past exams/Resits/2026-07 Resit (with solutions).pdf>) p.8 | Verbal ≤2 sent., 2 | 1 pt: the cost falls on *other* consumers. 1 pt: the individual does not take it into account when choosing Q. |
| [R26 1c](<../../Past exams/Resits/2026-07 Resit (with solutions).pdf>) p.2 | Verbal ≤3 sent., 3 | The subsidy = Ben's MU at Q* (the marginal external benefit Anne ignores). A higher b means a larger ignored benefit at the margin, so a larger subsidy is needed. |
| [R25 1d](<../../Past exams/Resits/2025-07 Resit (with solutions).pdf>) p.2 | Verbal ≤3 sent., 2 | With a binary choice (car/bike) only the *relative* cost matters, so a car tax and a bike subsidy are equivalent and both reach the efficient split. |
| [M25 2a](<../../Past exams/Midterms/2025 Midterm (with solutions).pdf>) p.2 | Numerical, 1 | Private q = k vs efficient q = k − c. The profit loss = triangle ½c² (Coase transfer; see [PUB-EXT-COASE](PUB-EXT-COASE.md)). |

**Pitfalls:**
- The tax is MEC **at Q\***.
- In welfare comparisons include **CS + PS − EC + tax revenue** (rule 9).
- Verbal parts need "*others*" plus "*not taken into account*". "Because there's an externality" alone scores little.

## 5. Book discussion questions
- **MUST 5.12** ([ch 5](<../../Textbooks/Rosen & Gayer (md)/05 - Externalities.md>), assigned W2). MB = 10 − X, MC = 5, external cost 2. *Hint:* market MB = MPC; efficient MB = MPC + 2; DWL triangle; tax = MEC at Q*; revenue = t·Q*.
- **MUST 5.4** (assigned W2; the figure is on pdf p. 186). Cassanova's parties, external benefit b. *Hint:* MSB = MB_p + b, Q* where MSB = MC, subsidy = b at Q*, then who gains vs taxpayers.
- **MUST 15.12** ([ch 15](<../../Textbooks/Rosen & Gayer (md)/15 - Taxation and Efficiency.md>), assigned W5). Liquor $1 tax EB, then MEC $0.50. *Hint:* EB = ½·t·ΔQ, and the avoided external cost 0.5·ΔQ offsets part of it. Exact twin of R26 3d.
- **MUST 9.6** ([ch 9](<../../Textbooks/Rosen & Gayer (md)/09 - The Health Care Market.md>), assigned W3). Doctor visits, coinsurance DWL, then MEB = 50. *Hint:* efficient P = MC; insured pays 0.5·MC; DWL triangle; with MEB the social MB shifts up.
- USEFUL 5.6 (fat tax targets an input), 5.11 (hog-farm table), 5.5 (mall rents).
