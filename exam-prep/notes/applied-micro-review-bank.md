## W1-03 — Purchases versus consumption

Status: clarified repeatedly; latest verbal confirmation of understanding, independent retention untested.

Rule: each consumes the entire non-rival total G=g_A+g_B. Symmetric purchases may split that total equally; consumption does not. Averaging reports is a provision rule, not a division of consumption. Private consumption follows each person's own budget.
