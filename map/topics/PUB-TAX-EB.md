## 1. How the exam asks it
Templates: **T2** step 2 (EB triangle), **T2** step 5 (a tax under an externality), **T7** step 3 (EV method), **T4** step 1 (EB as a project cost).

| Exam Q | Type, pts | Solution steps |
|---|---|---|
| [F24 7a](<../../Past exams/Finals/2024-10 Final (with solutions).pdf>) p.11 | Numerical, 1 | 1) Q without and with the €50 tax. 2) EB = ½·t·ΔQ = 5000 (or ½·ε·P·Q·t²). |
| [R25 3b](<../../Past exams/Resits/2025-07 Resit (with solutions).pdf>) p.6 | Numerical, 2 | 1) Perfectly elastic supply, so the consumer price rises by the full t. 2) ΔQ from demand. 3) EB = ½·5·ΔQ = 50. |
| [F25 9a](<../../Past exams/Finals/2025-10 Final (with solutions).pdf>) p.16 | Numerical, 2 | 1) Equilibria with and without the €30 tax. 2) EB = ½·30·ΔQ = 150. |
| [R26 3d](<../../Past exams/Resits/2026-07 Resit (with solutions).pdf>) p.8 | Numerical, 2 | With an externality: compare CS + PS + revenue − EC with and without the tax: +800. The "EB" is more than offset by the avoided external cost. (Shared with PIGOU.) |
| [R26 2c](<../../Past exams/Resits/2026-07 Resit (with solutions).pdf>) p.4 | Describe method, 2 | 1) Grant received. 2) Utility under the programme. 3) EV = the lump sum giving that utility. 4) Loss = grant − EV. Naming the terms is not enough. (Shared with REDIST-PROG.) |

**Pitfalls:**
- Use ΔQ between the **untaxed and taxed** equilibria.
- The revenue is a transfer, not a loss.
- With an external cost, the welfare effect of a tax ≠ −EB: include EC and revenue (R26 3d).
- EB is a **cost** in a CBA (F24 7b, see [PUB-CBA](PUB-CBA.md)).
