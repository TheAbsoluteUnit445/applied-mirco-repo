## 1. How the exam asks it
Template: **T1** steps 3-5 (Nash best responses, corner, consumption vs provision, add-ons).

| Exam Q | Type, pts | Solution steps |
|---|---|---|
| [M23 1c](<../../Past exams/Midterms/2023 Midterm (with solutions).pdf>) p.2 | Numerical, 1 | 1) BR_A: MRS_A(G_A + G_B) = p_G/p_x. 2) BR_B likewise. 3) Solve the 2×2 system: G_A = G_B = y/(3p_G). |
| [M25 1b](<../../Past exams/Midterms/2025 Midterm (with solutions).pdf>) p.1 | Numerical + reasoning, 1 | 1) The high-valuer A buys until MU_A = c, so Q = a − c. 2) At that Q, MU_B < c, so B buys 0. 3) **B consumes a − c** (non-rival). |
| [R26 1b](<../../Past exams/Resits/2026-07 Resit (with solutions).pdf>) p.2 | Numerical + reasoning, 2 | Same as M25 1b. "Ben consumes 0" = 1 of 2. Ben buying anything (e.g. b − c) = 0. |
| [F25 7d](<../../Past exams/Finals/2025-10 Final (with solutions).pdf>) p.13 | Numerical + reasoning, 2 | 1) D's MRS < MRT even at y = 0, so D contributes 0 (corner). 2) C sets MRS_C = MRT, giving y = 4. 3) State the provision of each. |
| [F24 5b](<../../Past exams/Finals/2024-10 Final (with solutions).pdf>) p.7 | Verbal, 1 | The provider (Anne) counts only her *own* MU α, so both the private and efficient Q shift with α, and the loss only depends on the ignored MUs of Ben and Dikra (b + d). |
| [F24 5c](<../../Past exams/Finals/2024-10 Final (with solutions).pdf>) p.7 | Numerical, 1 | 1) Bilateral efficient Q with Anne + Dikra. 2) Dikra's maximum payment = her gain on the **increase** from α/λ, giving d²/λ. (Shared with COASE.) |
| [R26 1c](<../../Past exams/Resits/2026-07 Resit (with solutions).pdf>) p.2 | Verbal ≤3 sent., 3 | The Pigouvian subsidy = Ben's MU at Q* (the marginal external benefit Anne ignores). A higher b means a bigger ignored benefit, so a bigger subsidy. (Shared with PIGOU.) |

**Pitfalls:**
- **Consumes ≠ provides**: always state what the free rider *consumes*.
- Derive **both** best responses **and** solve the system; stopping at the BRs = 0 (M23 1c).
- Bargaining pays only for the *increase* in Q (F24 5c: paying for all Q = 0).
