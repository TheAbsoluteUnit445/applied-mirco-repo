# Public Economics tutorial exercises: map by topic

Scope: all Additional Exercises in `Public Economics (Delfgaauw)/Week 1..5/Exercises/*.md`, the student's own Week 2 question docs, and the lectures for weeks 1-6 (`.../Lectures/*.md`). There is no Week 6 exercise sheet yet. Topic IDs are from `map/TOPICS.md`. Exam references are from `map/_work/exam-inventory.md`.

**Exam labels used below:** `2023-09M` = Midterm 20 Sept 2023, `2025-09M` = Midterm 17 Sept 2025, `2024-10` = Final 25 Oct 2024, `2025-07` = Resit 8 July 2025, `2025-10` = Final 24 Oct 2025, `2026-07` = Resit 7 July 2026. "(Pers)" marks a Personnel question that uses the same technique. "loose" means the technique matches but the setting differs.

**Solutions:** every official sheet (weeks 1-5) has an "Answers to exercises" section that covers all Additional Exercises and all assigned R&G discussion questions. The student's Week 2 docs (`Week 2 - All questions.md`, `... (with figures).md`, `Week 2 - Rosen Ch5 discussion questions.md`) only restate the Week 2 sheet: they add no new questions and no solutions, and they point back to the official sheet for answers.

## Lecture coverage per week

**Week 1 (Lecture 1 Introduction; Lecture 1.5 Review of perfect competition).** Covers PUB-WELF, PUB-SWF, PUB-POSNORM, PUB-PG-CLASS, PUB-PG-SAM, PUB-PG-NASH and PUB-CBA.
Not trained by the week 1 exercises:
- Edgeworth box, contract curve and the welfare theorems (only R&G DQ 3.x)
- Rawlsian vs utilitarian social indifference curves and the UPC (only DQ 3.4/3.6)
- Large-N free riding (G = V/p vs NV/p; efficiency loss rises in N)
- Lindahl / personalised prices and preference revelation
- Road pricing as rivalry under congestion
- Public vs private provision and production trade-offs (verbal)
- CBA: discounting, valuing intangibles (hedonic pricing), value of life and QALYs. Discounting is trained only by DQ 8.5 in week 4.
The government-failure list (information, Arrow, rent seeking, incentives) is only touched by Ex 1.3.

**Week 2 (Lecture 2 Externalities; Lecture 2.5 Applications, plus the "(pre)" version, which is a subset).** Covers PUB-EXT-GAME, PUB-EXT-PIGOU, PUB-EXT-COASE, PUB-EXT-INSTR, PUB-EXT-COMMONS and PUB-EDU.
Not trained:
