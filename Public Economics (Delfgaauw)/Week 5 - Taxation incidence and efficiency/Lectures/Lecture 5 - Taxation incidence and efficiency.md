This helps to understand Excess Burden:
Beer tax increases price of beer, which has two effects
1.
Consumer can buy less goods (income-effect)
2.
Relative price of beer increases  consumer shifts
consumption away from beer towards other, non-taxed
goods (or leisure)  (substitution-effect)

 This shift makes that tax revenue < utility loss
In other words:
Through substitution effect, taxes distort behaviour
These distortions lead to Excess burden.

Is an income tax a lump-sum tax?
Answer: no!
 Income can be affected: choice between income and leisure
If Y = income; T = max hours; w = wage, then
Budget constraint without income tax
Y = [T – L]w
Budget constraint with (proportional) income tax t
Y = [T – L](1 – t)w
 The higher t, the more attractive leisure relative to work
