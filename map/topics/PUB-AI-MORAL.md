# PUB-AI-MORAL: Moral hazard, insurance design, health care market

**Status:** taught (W3) | **Exam weight:** 0 of 6 papers | **Priority: C** (skim; the DWL-of-coinsurance triangle is the same skill as PIGOU/EB)

## 1. How the exam asks it
- Never examined directly.
- Nearest: R26 4a uses the same expected-utility-with-√Y comparison as Ex 3.3a-b (see [PUB-REDIST-PROG](PUB-REDIST-PROG.md)).
- Moral hazard is examined in **Personnel** (effort under a linear contract, T10, and R26 8a "moral hazard + adverse selection"). Knowing the definition pays there.
- If asked in Public, expect a verbal part: "full insurance kills the incentive for precaution, because the insured no longer bears the loss", or a coinsurance DWL triangle.
