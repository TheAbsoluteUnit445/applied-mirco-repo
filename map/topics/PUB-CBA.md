Exam page numbers (p.) are PDF pages of the solutions file. Templates: see [exams-tagged.md](<../_work/exams-tagged.md>) §3.

## 1. How the exam asks it
Template: **T4** (weighted-SWF project appraisal). Discounting, shadow prices and the value of life have **never** been asked.

| Exam Q | Type, pts | Solution steps |
|---|---|---|
| [F24 7b](<../../Past exams/Finals/2024-10 Final (with solutions).pdf>) p.11 | Numerical, 2 | 1) Cost to the rich = tax revenue 20000 **+ EB 5000** (EB from 7a). 2) Weighted benefits: α·B_poor + B_rich. 3) Project passes iff the weighted benefits exceed the weighted costs, so α > 1.25. 1 pt for the totals, 1 pt for the inequality. |
| [R25 3e](<../../Past exams/Resits/2025-07 Resit (with solutions).pdf>) p.6 | Numerical, 2 | 1) Lump-sum dyke: each group's net = benefit − 20. 2) The poor are net *losers* (15 − 20). 3) Weighted sum > 0 gives α < 2. |

**Pitfalls:**
- Forgetting the EB when the project is financed by a distortionary tax (the F24 trap).
- Check who gains net before reading "higher weight on the poor helps".
- A lump-sum-financed project with total B > C is *efficient* but can still fail the Pareto test (see [PUB-WELF](PUB-WELF.md)).
