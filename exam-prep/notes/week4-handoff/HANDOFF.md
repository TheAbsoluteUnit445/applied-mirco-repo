## Recurring errors worth targeting

1. Confusing income before transfer, disposable income, transfer received and transfer paid; subtracting transfers twice.
2. Forgetting to square coefficients when removing square roots; collecting G + 0.25G as 2G rather than 1.25G.
3. Dropping utility/SWF coefficients and concluding equal incomes automatically.
4. Describing algebra instead of the economic mechanism; changing income when the comparative static changes a weight with income fixed.
5. Nash objective: differentiate own gift holding the other's fixed. Each donor counts their own altruistic benefit but ignores the benefit of their gift to the other donor.
6. Distinguish an optimal gift from a Pareto comparison of a specified gift. One person's loss rules out a Pareto improvement even if total utility rises.
7. Welfare budget: leisure opportunity cost is the wage times the retained earnings share; benefit is not total income. Normality of leisure, rather than diminishing marginal utility alone, establishes the relevant income effect.
