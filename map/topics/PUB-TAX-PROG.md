## 1. How the exam asks it
Template: **T5** step 4 (Ramsey + progressivity).

| Exam Q | Type, pts | Solution steps |
|---|---|---|
| [F25 10b](<../../Past exams/Finals/2025-10 Final (with solutions).pdf>) p.18 | Verbal 1 sent., 1 | 1) Progressivity is judged by the **average** tax rate as income rises. 2) Under Ramsey, the poor's inelastic good gets the highest rate, so the poor pay a higher share of income. 3) The system is **regressive**. |

Likely variants (from the lecture and the book): "Is T = −a + tI progressive?" (yes iff the ATR rises in I, i.e. a lump-sum grant a > 0 with a flat t). "Is a flat tax progressive?" (only with an exemption or grant).

**Pitfalls:**
- Use the *average*, not the marginal, rate.
- Give a one-sentence channel (who consumes the heavily taxed good), not a definition.
