Work Incentives: mathematical example
Now: benefit program.
Maximum grant (in case of zero earnings) G = 450 euro.
Each euro in earnings reduces benefit B by 10 cents: t = 0.1
This implies B = 0 when earnings ≥ 4500
For Bob, this occurs when hours worked ≥ 4500/15 = 300; or when L ≤ 720 – 300 = 420

Bob’s new budget constraint:
For L ≤ 420 equal to old: Y = 10800 – 15L
For L > 420: increase in L by 1 reduces Y by (0.9)x15 = 13.5
In math: Y = 450 + 13.5 (720 – L)  Y = 10170 – 13.5L
