## Slide 2

Principal-agent relationships
Definitions:
If I hire or contract you to take some action or make decisions on my behalf, then I am the principal, and you are the agent.
Principal agent relationship occurs when a principal hires or contracts an agent to take actions or make decisions that affect the principal
Example: shareholders (principals) hire a CEO (agent) to run their firm


<!-- slide 3 -->
## Slide 3

Principal-agent relationships
Why important?
Prevalent in the economy and within firms
Can lead to economic inefficiencies/problems

Principal-agent problems:
The objectives of the principal and agent differ
When the actions taken by the agent (shirking) or information possessed by the agent are hard to observe (asymmetric information)


<!-- slide 4 -->
## Slide 4

Principal-agent relationships
Example 1: Shareholder & CEO
Principal: Shareholders
Agent: CEO
Differing objectives?
Shareholders: Maximize profit
CEOs: Maximize personal wealth/profit/career
Shirking/information asymmetries?
Shareholders unable to observe all actions and behaviour of CEO


<!-- slide 5 -->
## Slide 5

Principal-agent relationships
Example 2: Voter & Politician
Principal: Voters
Agent: Politician
Differing objectives?
Voters : Certain policy goals/interests
Politician : Career/re-election/wealth…
Shirking/information asymmetries?
Effort and behavior behind doors hard for voters to observe
