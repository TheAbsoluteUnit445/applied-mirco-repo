**Status:** taught (W2, Lecture 2.5) | **Exam weight:** 0 of 6 papers | **Priority: C** (skim)

## 1. How the exam asks it
- Never examined directly.
- The technique (a positive externality, so a Pigouvian subsidy = MEB at q*) is examined under [PUB-EXT-PIGOU](PUB-EXT-PIGOU.md) (R26 1c, R26 3d logic).
- If it ever appears, expect a 2-sentence verbal question: "why does the government subsidise education?" The answer is the positive externality (social > private benefit) and capital-market imperfections (poor students can't borrow). Equity is a *normative* reason.
