1 pt
Coase Theorem implies that person A produces the socially efficient level of q rather than the
profit-maximising level of q. Person A only accepts to reduce production if the resulting reduction
in profit is compensated for by the transfer T. The privately-optimal q is given by maximizing profit
with respect to q. This gives first-order condition k – q = 0, so that q = k. This yields profit ½ k2.
Socially efficient level of q is given by maximizing profit minus external cost. This yields first-order
condition k – q – c = 0, so that q* = k - c. This yields profit k*(k-c) – ½ (k-c)2. Hence, the minimal T
= ½ k2 – [k*(k-c) – ½ (k-c)2] = ½ c2. The final step can also be done (implicitly via a graph) as
(1/2)*[k - (k-c)]*c or using an integral.
