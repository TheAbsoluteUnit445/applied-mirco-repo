# PER-PA-EFFDIST - Efficiency vs distribution, rent extraction, dominated contracts

**Status:** taught (lecture 2, Sets 2 and 4) · **Exam weight:** in **2/4** finals/resits (R25, R26) + M23 · 3 F/R sub-Qs · **1.6% of a typical exam** (split) · **Priority A** (the verbal and efficiency add-on to every T10 block)

## How the exam asks it

Template **T10, steps 4-6**.

| Exam Q | Type | Content |
|---|---|---|
| [M23 3d](<../../Past exams/Midterms/2023 Midterm (with solutions).pdf>) | Verbal (derivation optional) | Why a base salary raises the optimal b: the bonus paid on K is clawed back through a lower a (rent extraction) |
| [R25 4c](<../../Past exams/Resits/2025-07 Resit (with solutions).pdf>) | Numerical | b = p with a two-part contract |
| [R25 4d](<../../Past exams/Resits/2025-07 Resit (with solutions).pdf>) | Verbal (max 2 sentences) | Two reasons lower guilt raises profit: the PC is cheaper, and effort is higher |
| [R26 6a](<../../Past exams/Resits/2026-07 Resit (with solutions).pdf>) | Numerical | efficient e = (p + γ)/θ: maximise U + Π, the bq terms cancel |

**Steps (efficiency):**
1. Social surplus = U + Π.
2. The transfers (a and bq) cancel.
3. Take the FOC in e to get the efficient e*.
4. Compare with the agent's e(b). Efficiency requires b = p (or b = 1 per unit of output).
5. Distribution is set by a alone.

**Pitfalls:** 1 pt for showing the cancellation and 1 pt for solving (R26 6a). In verbal answers, name the *rent-extraction* mechanism, not the formula.
